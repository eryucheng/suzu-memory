Assess whether the fixed subject has the specified real-world condition and how it constrains, enables or explains behavior.

targetMatch distinguishes the condition itself, contextual relevance, none and unknown. Use explicit_self_report for the subject's report, direct_observation for a direct record, reported_by_other for a report, and inferred for inference. present means the condition exists; absent requires explicit absence or removal. Silence leaves it unknown.

Preserve current, historical, temporary, future or unknown time. effect requires evidence. revisionCue is none for an ordinary report, started for onset, ended for removal or ending, changed for change, clarified for scope qualification, denies_prior_state for denied prior attribution, and unknown when unclear. Keep conditions and frequent behavior within their scope without turning them into preferences or permanent personality.
