Identify whether the goal is future, active, in progress, blocked, paused, completed, cancelled, abandoned, historical or unknown, preserving evidence of closure.

completed requires an explicit completion report or direct result; a guess is inferred. cancelled/abandoned requires explicit cancellation, abandonment or release. Preserve blocking, pausing and uncertainty separately. Elapsed expected time, starting a tool or silence does not establish closure.

future can describe an established plan scheduled for later; historical describes a recalled past status. Keep timeReference as the source's readable time expression.
