Identify whether the association is current, historical, temporary, future or unknown, and any explicit strengthening, weakening, extinction, changed emotion, scope clarification, denial of prior attribution or absence of change.

clarified requires a narrowed trigger scope or added condition. denies_prior_state requires denying the earlier association. extinguished requires an explicit same-scope statement that the trigger no longer causes the feeling. Silence, a brief lack of reaction, one different feeling or conflicting content alone does not establish change.
