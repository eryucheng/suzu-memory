Distinguish stable self-definition, reflective reinterpretation, temporary self-appraisal, situational role, objective identity fact and no self-concept. Preserve evidence such as repeated reflection, a turning-point event, one reflection, acute emotion, acting or a factual record.

Self-criticism or boasting during acute emotion may be temporary_self_appraisal + acute_emotion; judge the context rather than treating every statement after failure or excitement as temporary. A turning-point event supports reinterpretation only when accompanied by the subject's reflection.
