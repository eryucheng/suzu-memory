Assess a single, repeated, habitual, interrupted or stopped pattern in the fixed subject's specified behavior, preserving context and constraints.

single is one occurrence; repeated/habitual requires an explicit repetition statement or multiple records; interrupted means disrupted without established permanent cessation; stopped requires explicit cessation. Repeated behavior under work, institutional, health or resource constraints may still be a routine. Preserve those constraints without inferring liking or personality.

Use explicit_self_report for self-report, direct_observation for direct records, reported_by_other for reports and inferred for inference. revisionCue is none, changed, clarified, interrupted, stopped, denies_prior_state or unknown according to explicit cues. Silence or conflict alone does not establish changed habits.
