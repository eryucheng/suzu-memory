Distinguish an explicit principle, reasoned priority, costly voluntary choice, ordinary choice, constrained behavior, instrumental behavior, aspirational slogan and no tradeoff. Assess alternatives, agency, cost and whether the protected object matches the fixed value independently.

costly_choice requires feasible alternatives, voluntary choice, identifiable cost and actual protection of that value. Behavior forced by work, survival, institutions, punishment or ability constraints is constrained_behavior. A step toward another end is instrumental_behavior. Time spent alone does not establish a costly choice.

Explicit principles and value priorities are evidence in their own right, without requiring action or cost. Keep their evidence type distinct from demonstrated tradeoffs.
