Assess the fixed subject's behavior toward the specified object: agency, constraints, actual alternatives, instrumental purpose, opportunity cost and ability to decline.

Record work, survival, institutional, pressure, resource and convenience conditions separately. available requires feasible alternatives at the time; canDecline=yes requires evidence of the ability to refuse. Frequency and time spent alone do not establish agency or voluntary cost. choice, acceptance, avoidance, exposure and routine describe behavior forms only.

Unknown conditions do not negate explicit preferences. Liking while avoiding and disliking while participating can coexist. confidence concerns this task's classifications.
