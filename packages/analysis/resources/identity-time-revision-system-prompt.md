Identify current, historical, timeless, future, temporary or unknown identity facts, and explicit onset, change, ending, clarification, denial of prior attribution or no change.

Use tense and supplied times: a recollection is historical and a future arrangement future. changed, started, ended, clarified and denies_prior_state require corresponding language cues. A new value or conflicting statement alone does not establish that an old value ended, especially for multi-value fields. Marital status follows the same rules.
