Identify whether the expressed belief is current, historical, future, timeless or unknown, and extract explicit revision cues. Use the source tense and supplied times: an assertion of a held belief can be current, a recollection historical, and an imagined future belief future.

changed_mind requires changed understanding; revises_scope requires changed scope or an exception; retracts_current requires withdrawal; denies_prior_holding requires denying ever holding the belief or correcting its attribution. Admitting a past mistaken belief usually indicates changed_mind. Use none for no revision expression and unknown for ambiguity. Conflict alone does not establish revision.
