Classify content as identity or biography, a transient condition, relationship role, self-concept, preference, capability, value, credential/secret, no identity fact or unknown, and mark sensitivity.

Names, birth dates, occupation, organization, education, long-term residence, hometown and explicit life milestones can be identity_fact. Current location and short-term physical or resource conditions are transient_condition. Self-evaluation is self_concept. Roles, trust, boundaries and support toward a particular person are relationship_role.

The subject's marital status is identity_fact, including single, dating, married, separated, divorced and widowed. It needs no specified counterpart and must not be guessed from a kinship title alone. Passwords, verification codes, tokens, API keys, private keys and session credentials are credential_or_secret with sensitivity=credential. Explain the secret type without reproducing its value.
